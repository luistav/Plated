# 👨‍🍳 Mise en Place: Chef R&D Engine Roadmap

Strategic vision for the evolution of the professional culinary development platform.

---

## ✅ Phase 1: The Foundation (Completed)
*   **Atomic Data Model:** Architecture for Ingredients, Suppliers, and Unit of Measure (UOM) management.
*   **Recursive Costing Engine:** Implementation of depth-first traversal for nested recipe costing.
*   **Multi-Tenant Privacy:** User-scoped Firestore silos to protect proprietary IP.
*   **Authentication:** Chef profile management and secure onboarding.

---

## 🏗 Phase 2: Intelligence & Documentation (Current)
*   **AI Multimodal OCR:** Gemini-powered invoice scanning to automate pricing updates.
*   **AI Creative Suite:** Automated naming conventions and marketing copy refinement for dish concepts.
*   **Visual Plating Guides:** Documentation of technical execution steps with image persistence.
*   **The Recipe Bible:** High-fidelity print optimization for physical kitchen binders.

---

## 🚀 Phase 3: Operational Scaling (Future - Q3 2025)
*   **Inventory Depletion:** Linking Dish sales to theoretical ingredient stock levels.
*   **Waste Tracking:** Real-time logging of kitchen prep waste with financial impact reporting.
*   **QR Code Deployment:** Generation of QR codes for prep containers that link directly to digital Recipe Specs.
*   **Allergen Matrix:** Automated cross-referencing of ingredient metadata to generate allergen warnings for menus.

---

## 🌐 Phase 4: Collaborative Ecosystem (Future - Q4 2025)
*   **Kitchen Brigade Access:** Sub-user roles for Sous Chefs and CDPs with limited financial visibility.
*   **Supplier API Integrations:** Direct real-time price feeds from major food distributors.
*   **Menu Engineering Heatmaps:** Visualizing GP% health against historical sales volume.
*   **Offline Mode:** Service worker implementation for kitchens with poor connectivity (basement prep areas).

---

## 💎 Phase 5: Predictive Intelligence (Future - 2026)
*   **Yield Variance Analysis:** AI prediction of prep yields based on seasonal quality data.
*   **Smart Procurement:** Automated ordering suggestions based on menu cycles and supplier delivery windows.
*   **Carbon Footprint Tracking:** Analysis of ingredient sourcing distances and sustainability metrics.
